Console — healthy

1 State leads: name, live badge, resource cards above the terminal — the operator verifies at a glance before acting.

2 Power controls are one tap. Restart stays cheap; Stop is the accent (stop is the destructive end of the power range — verify the actual hierarchy in /evaluate).

Console — daemon offline (degraded)

1 The failure names itself: not "error", but the daemon + node are unreachable. This is the Tier-1 degradation contract from /blueprint.

2 The boundary is explicit: what's blocked, and that the rest of the panel still works — no stranded operator.

3 An honest out-of-panel exit (the daemon service command) beats a dead end — the multi-channel handoff from /journey.

Console — crash diagnosis

1 The last-known failure reason is a status line, not buried in logs: "Exit code 1" plus the count ("3 times in 5 minutes") — lead-with-state under pressure.

2 Reinstall is offered inline at the failure point, framed honestly (world + config preserved). Open question: does reinstall actually preserve them? /fortify to verify.
